      AI providers
      
        
      rmm's own AI runs on a provider you pay for, with your API key. Keys are kept in your user folder, never in the instance.
      
        
      
        
      Mod team
      
        
      Who answers in the AI tab. A message with a role goes to that role's AI, and the Lead hands each step to the AI of its role. An AI app such as Claude can take a role over MCP ( or rmm mcp): steps for it wait on the team board until you ask the app to do its rmm team tasks, and it can hand steps to the roles on a provider here.
